Delete your data

Groowl by Learnion · Updated 7 November 2026

In short

  • Groowl has no accounts. Your diary lives only on your phone, so you delete it yourself.
  • For crash reports, email us and we delete them.
  • Anything left is deleted automatically, or is held by Google under its own policy.

Your diary (meals, symptoms, check-ins, gut profile)

We don't have a copy, so only you can delete it. Pick one:

  • One entry: open it in the app and tap Delete.
  • Everything, keep the app: on your phone open Settings > Apps > Groowl > Storage and tap Clear storage.
  • Everything: uninstall Groowl.

Deleting is permanent. If you exported your diary to a file, delete that file too. If you want to keep a copy first, use Export and import in the app.

Crash reports

Turn off Send crash reports in the app to stop sending them. To delete the ones already sent, email learnion.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Groowl data deletion" and your phone model plus the rough date of the crash, so we can find your reports. We delete them within 30 days. If you don't write, Firebase Crashlytics deletes crash data automatically after 90 days.

AI requests

When you tap an AI button, the meal details go to Google's Gemini. We don't store them anywhere except your phone, so deleting your diary deletes our copy. Google may keep the request under the Gemini API terms. Because Groowl has no accounts, we can't link a request to you, so contact Google about what it keeps.

Ads

AdMob, run by Google, may keep device identifiers and ad interactions. To cut that link, open Settings > Google > Ads on your phone and reset or delete your advertising ID. You can also change your choice in the app under Settings > Ad privacy options. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Questions

Email learnion.dev@gmail.com. More detail is in our privacy policy.
