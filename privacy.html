AI features

AI is used only when you tap Enhance with AI or Autofill with AI. Then the app sends the meal or dish name, its ingredients and short notes from your log and gut profile to Google's Gemini model, which writes the reply. Nothing is sent if you don't tap. While Groowl uses Google's free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products.

Ads are optional rewarded videos from AdMob that you choose to watch for extra AI tickets. They are not personalized, and no diary data is sent to AdMob. Where required, Google's consent form is shown before the first ad, and you can change your choice in Settings > Ad privacy options.
