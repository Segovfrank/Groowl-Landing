Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short

  • Your diary stays on your phone. We have no account system, no server and no copy of your diary.
  • Meal and symptom details are sent to Google's Gemini AI only when you tap an AI button.
  • Ads are optional and not personalized. Crash reports are sent only if you turn them on.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who is responsible

Groowl is made by Learnion, operated by Francisco Segovia, an independent developer based in Mexico. Francisco Segovia is responsible for your personal data under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP), and is the data controller under the EU and UK GDPR. Contact: learnion.dev@gmail.com.

Your diary

The meals, symptoms, gut profile answers and notes you log are stored only on your device. We don't have a copy, and they are not included in Android's automatic backup. You can export or delete them in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

AI features and health data

Symptoms and gut profile answers are health data, which the law treats as sensitive. AI is used only when you tap Enhance with AI or Autofill with AI. Then the app sends the meal or dish name, its ingredients and short notes from your log and gut profile to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic, and Gemini writes the reply. Nothing is sent if you don't tap.

Our legal basis is your explicit consent, which you give each time you tap an AI button. You can withdraw it by not using the AI features; the rest of the app works without them. Groowl uses Gemini's free tier, so Google may keep this content and use it to improve its products, as described in the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy. Don't put your name or other identifying details in meal notes. We don't store AI requests or replies anywhere other than your phone.

Ads

Ads are optional rewarded videos from AdMob that you choose to watch for extra AI tickets. They are not personalized, and no diary data is sent to AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, your IP address and ad interactions to show ads and prevent fraud. Where required, Google's consent form is shown before the first ad, and you can change your choice in Settings > Ad privacy options. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Crash reports

Crash reports are sent to Firebase Crashlytics only if you turn on Send crash reports. They include your device model, Android version, app version, the time of the crash, technical details of the error and an installation identifier. They never include your diary. Our legal basis is your consent, and you can turn the setting off at any time. Crashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days. See the Firebase privacy information.

This website

groowl.app has no analytics, no cookies and no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

International transfers

Google and GitHub may process the data described above on servers in the United States and other countries. They protect it under their own data processing terms, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses where they apply.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. In Mexico these are your ARCO rights. Because your diary never leaves your phone, you control it directly in the app. For data held by Google (AI requests, ads, crash reports), email us and we will help, or contact Google directly. Step by step: how to delete your data. We will reply within the time the law requires.

You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Mexico that is the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno; in the EU or UK, your national supervisory authority.

We don't sell or share personal data for cross-context advertising, as those terms are defined by California law.

Security

Data sent to Google travels over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Keep your phone locked and up to date.

Children

Groowl is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the AI features or sent a crash report, contact us and we will help get it deleted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and tell you in the app if a change significantly affects how your data is used.
