Food data

Open Food Facts

Groowl's on-device food models read meal names through a list of 20,000 words built from Open Food Facts product names. Because the list comes from their database, we publish it under the same licence.

  • vocab.txt the word list, 173 KB
  • vocab.LICENCE.txt its licence
